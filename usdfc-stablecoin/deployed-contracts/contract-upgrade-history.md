# Contract Upgrade History

## 25 September 2026 - Filecoin oracle update

Following the discontinuation of Pyth price oracle support on Filecoin, USDFC migrated to RedStone as its primary oracle. The update also included a contract upgrade to adjust parameters.

### Execution status

Both transactions were successfully executed through the Safe multisig wallet on Filecoin. All times below are UTC.
