# 📣 Campaign

## Prerequisites

- A connected wallet
- Access to the Secured Finance platform
- Basic understanding of the platform's features

## Overview

The [**Campaign page**](https://app.secured.finance/campaign/) features current and upcoming promotions where you can earn additional benefits and [**points**](points.md) by completing specific activities on the platform. Campaigns are time-limited events designed to incentivize participation and boost platform engagement.

## Step 1: Accessing the Campaign Page

1. Navigate to the [Secured Finance platform](https://app.secured.finance/)
2. Connect your wallet if not already connected
3. Click on the "Campaign" tab in the main navigation
4. The page will load with all active and upcoming campaigns

## Step 2: Understanding Campaign Components

Each campaign includes several key components:

1. **Campaign Details**
   - Name and description of the campaign
   - Specific objectives and goals
   - Visual elements representing the campaign theme

2. **Duration**
   - Start and end dates for the campaign
   - Countdown timer showing time remaining
   - Campaign status (Active, Upcoming, or Completed)

3. **Quests and Rewards**
   - List of specific tasks or challenges to complete
   - Points or rewards associated with each quest
   - Requirements for completing each quest

4. **Progress Tracking**
   - Total Value Locked (TVL) metrics
   - Community progress toward campaign goals
   - Your personal contribution to the campaign

![Campaign Page](../../../.gitbook/assets/campaign-page.png)

## Step 3: Participating in a Campaign

1. Review the active campaigns and their requirements
2. Select a campaign you want to participate in
3. Click on the campaign card to view detailed information
4. Review the specific quests and rewards
5. Begin completing the required activities

## Step 4: Completing Campaign Quests

1. Navigate to the specific section of the platform required for the quest
2. Complete the required action (e.g., deposit assets, place orders, etc.)
3. Return to the Campaign page to check if the quest has been marked as completed
4. Continue with other quests to maximize your rewards

## Step 5: Claiming Rewards

1. Once you've completed campaign quests, check if rewards are:
   - Automatically distributed to your account
   - Requiring manual claiming
2. If manual claiming is required:
   - Click the "Claim" button on the completed quest
   - Confirm the transaction in your wallet
   - Wait for the transaction to be confirmed on the blockchain

## Next Steps

After participating in campaigns, you might want to:

- [Check your points balance](points.md) to see your accumulated rewards
- [Explore the leaderboard](points.md) to see how you rank against other participants
- [Join our community channels](../../../community-and-support/README.md) to stay updated on future campaigns

## Troubleshooting

### Quest Not Registering as Completed

If your quest activity isn't being recognized:
- Ensure the transaction was successfully confirmed on the blockchain
- Check that you've met all the specific requirements for the quest
- There may be a delay in updating quest status; try refreshing the page
- Verify that the campaign is still active

### Unable to Claim Rewards

If you're having trouble claiming rewards:
- Ensure you have enough native tokens for gas fees
- Check that the campaign hasn't expired
- Verify that you've completed all prerequisites for the reward
- Try refreshing the page and attempting again

### Campaign Not Displaying

If campaigns aren't displaying:
- Ensure your wallet is properly connected
- Check that you're on the correct network
- Clear your browser cache and try again
- Some campaigns may be region-specific or require specific eligibility criteria
